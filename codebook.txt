ADULT INCOME CLASSIFICATION CODEBOOK
====================================

Source
------
The CSV is a Kaggle-hosted copy of the Adult dataset from the UCI Machine
Learning Repository. The data were extracted from the 1994 United States
Census database. Missing categorical values are represented by "?" in the
raw CSV and are imported as NA.

Outcome encoding in the processed data
--------------------------------------
high      Annual income is greater than $50,000.
not_high  Annual income is at most $50,000.

Raw variables
-------------
age
  Integer. Age in years. Retained as a demographic predictor.

workclass
  Categorical. Employment sector or work arrangement. Raw levels:
  Federal-gov, Local-gov, Never-worked, Private, Self-emp-inc,
  Self-emp-not-inc, State-gov, Without-pay, and ? (missing). Retained.

fnlwgt
  Integer. Census final sampling weight. Excluded because it is a survey
  weight rather than an individual demographic, educational, or employment
  characteristic.

education
  Categorical. Highest educational credential, with levels Preschool,
  1st-4th, 5th-6th, 7th-8th, 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, HS-grad,
  Some-college, Assoc-voc, Assoc-acdm, Bachelors, Masters, Prof-school,
  and Doctorate. Excluded because education_num contains the same ordered
  information.

education.num / education_num
  Integer from 1 through 16. Ordered encoding of education, from Preschool
  through Doctorate. Retained as an educational predictor.

marital.status / marital_status
  Categorical. Divorced, Married-AF-spouse, Married-civ-spouse,
  Married-spouse-absent, Never-married, Separated, or Widowed. Retained.

occupation
  Categorical. Adm-clerical, Armed-Forces, Craft-repair, Exec-managerial,
  Farming-fishing, Handlers-cleaners, Machine-op-inspct, Other-service,
  Priv-house-serv, Prof-specialty, Protective-serv, Sales, Tech-support,
  Transport-moving, or ? (missing). Retained.

relationship
  Categorical. Husband, Not-in-family, Other-relative, Own-child, Unmarried,
  or Wife. Describes relationship to the household reference person. Retained.

race
  Categorical. Amer-Indian-Eskimo, Asian-Pac-Islander, Black, Other, or White.
  Retained as a demographic predictor; interpretation requires ethical care.

sex
  Categorical. Female or Male in the original data. Retained as a demographic
  predictor; interpretation requires ethical care.

capital.gain / capital_gain
  Integer. Annual capital gains in US dollars. Zero means no recorded gain.
  Retained as a financial/employment-related predictor.

capital.loss / capital_loss
  Integer. Annual capital losses in US dollars. Zero means no recorded loss.
  Retained as a financial/employment-related predictor.

hours.per.week / hours_per_week
  Integer. Usual number of hours worked per week. Retained.

native.country / native_country
  Categorical. Country of birth. The raw data contain 41 observed countries
  plus ? for missing values. Rare countries are combined within modeling
  recipes, separately inside each resample. Retained as a demographic
  predictor.

income
  Binary categorical response. Raw values are <=50K and >50K. Processed values
  are not_high and high, respectively. high is the positive event.

Processed datasets
------------------
adult_clean.csv contains 30,139 complete, unique observations, the binary
outcome, and 12 retained predictors. Rows containing missing values are
removed. Exact duplicate raw records are removed before complete-case
selection.
